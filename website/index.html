02 / ORBITAL EVIDENCE

One landing site. Several stories.

NASA’s Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter captured the landing area from above. The annotated view identifies the major pieces of the Mars 2020 landing system.

Read the landscape.

The rover is near the centre of the landing pattern. NASA describes a V-shaped debris pattern from the descent stage’s departure and identifies the separated heat shield and parachute/backshell elsewhere in the landing area. Parachute deceleration
