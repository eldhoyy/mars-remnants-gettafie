## Project name
MARS REMNANTS – Interactive Mars Hardware Explorer

## Summary (50–200 words)
MARS REMNANTS is an interactive educational explorer that reveals the engineering stories behind NASA hardware left on Mars after robotic missions. Using NASA’s orbital view of the Perseverance landing site and mission engineering documentation, the experience guides visitors through the parachute, backshell, heat shield and powered descent stage. Visitors can inspect evidence-linked hardware profiles, follow a mission timeline and test their understanding through short discovery activities. Designed for school-age learners, teachers, students and space enthusiasts, the project turns mission evidence into a clear visual story about how spacecraft reach another world—and why some components remain there. Its impact is to make planetary exploration more understandable while modelling careful use of primary scientific sources.
